Math Beat (drums)
i = step in the bar (0–15), n = bar number, v = variation 1–6. m(a,b)=a mod b, f=floor, e=exp, sin/cos/abs, r(i,n)=random 0–1. Result 0–1 = loudness (hats above 1 = open hat).
